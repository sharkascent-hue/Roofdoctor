FAQs

Questions we get asked.

How long does a new roof installation take?

Most new roof projects are completed within 2–5 days, depending on size and weather conditions.

How do I know if I need a new roof instead of repairs?

Signs include frequent leaks, missing shingles, sagging areas, or a roof older than 20–25 years. We offer inspections to help you decide.

What roofing materials do you recommend?

We work with slate, tile, asphalt shingles, and metal roofing—each with its own style, durability, and price range.

Is there a warranty on your new roof installations?

Yes, we offer both manufacturer warranties on materials and our own workmanship guarantee.
